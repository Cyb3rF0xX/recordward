Equifax (AnnualCreditReport)

DUE · never requested
every 30d last requested: never

Pull full report; review the Inquiries section.

What to do here
  1. Open the site and choose to request your report (you can do all three bureaus, but this card is for one — pull the matching one).
  2. Verify your identity (SSN, DOB, address; they may ask security questions).
  3. Open the report and go straight to the Inquiries section.
  4. Look under soft / promotional / employment inquiries for any name you don't recognize or any employer/screener.
  5. Save or screenshot the inquiries list so you can compare next time.
  6. Reports are free weekly, so pulling often is fine and never hurts your score.

LexisNexis Consumer Disclosure Report

DUE · never requested
every 90d last requested: never

Free FCRA disclosure. Consider also placing a security freeze.

What to do here
  1. Open the consumer disclosure request page.
  2. Submit a Full File Disclosure request (free under FCRA).
  3. Verify your identity; the report usually arrives by mail/secure delivery, not instantly.
  4. When it arrives, review it for background/employment data and any recipients listed.
  5. Optional but recommended: place a LexisNexis security freeze while you're there.

HireRight (file request)

DUE · never requested

Request file disclosure + recipient list.

What to do here
  1. Open the vendor's consumer / candidate portal (link below).
  2. Request a copy of your file disclosure (free under FCRA).
  3. Also request the list of recipients in the past 2 years — FCRA entitles you to know who pulled a report on you for employment purposes.
  4. Verify your identity as prompted.
  5. When the file arrives, check it for accuracy and for any recipient you don't recognize.
  6. If anything is wrong, use their dispute process to correct it.
