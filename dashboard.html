Equifax (AnnualCreditReport)

DUE · never requested
every 30d free limit: once per 7d last requested: never

Free weekly report. Review Inquiries; freeze, fraud alert and disputes are free.

What to do here
  1. Request: open AnnualCreditReport.com and pull the Equifax report. This card is for one bureau.
  2. Verify your identity (SSN, DOB, address; you may get security questions).
  3. Go straight to the Inquiries section and look for any name or employer/screener you don't recognize.
  4. Save or screenshot the inquiries list to compare next time. Reports are free weekly, and checking never hurts your score.
  5. Dispute anything wrong, incomplete or unrecognized. Disputes are free, the company must investigate (generally within 30 days), and you should keep dated copies of everything you send. Use the dispute link on Equifax's own site or report.
  6. Freeze: a security freeze is free by federal law and lifting it is free too. Place or lift it at Equifax.
  7. Fraud alert: free. A 1-year alert needs only a request; a 7-year extended alert needs an identity theft report from IdentityTheft.gov. Placing one at a bureau passes it to the other two.
  8. Opt out of prescreened credit/insurance offers at OptOutPrescreen.com.

LexisNexis Consumer Disclosure Report

DUE · never requested
every 365d free limit: once per 365d last requested: never

One free disclosure per 12 months. Freeze is free; opt-out form available.

What to do here
  1. Request: open the request form and ask for your Consumer Disclosure Report. One is free per 12 months. Phone: 1-888-497-0011.
  2. Verify your identity (SSN or driver's license). The report arrives by mail, not instantly.
  3. Review it for background/employment data and any recipients listed. You can also request a Description of Procedure letter on the same form.
  4. Dispute any inaccurate item with the LexisNexis Consumer Center (free). You can't have the whole file deleted, because it is a consumer reporting agency.
  5. Freeze: apply, lift or remove a security freeze at no charge.
  6. Opt out / opt in of marketing uses with the opt-out form (no SSN needed for this one).
